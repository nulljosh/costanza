# Costanza Privacy Policy

*Last updated November 2026*

Costanza is a poetry network. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What leaves your device
- **Your account.** Your email and hashed password are kept on our own server on Cloudflare. You can delete your account and every poem in it from the app or the site.
- **Public by design.** Poems you publish and your author name are public. That's the point.
- **Site analytics.** The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites. The apps have no analytics.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Costanza isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
