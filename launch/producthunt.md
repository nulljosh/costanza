Name (<=40 chars):
Co-Stanza

Tagline (<=60 chars):
Poetry with no likes and no follower count.

Description (<=260 chars):
A poetry network with no likes, no rankings and no follower counts. Sign in, pick a pen name and publish. Each poem gets its own page, each name gets a page with all its poems, and the front page is newest first. Free.

Topics (3):
Writing, Social Media, iOS

First comment (maker story):
Most poetry sites slide into a popularity contest. Likes, follows and feeds that reward whoever posts loudest, and then the poem is competing with its own numbers. Co-Stanza strips that back to the writing.

There is one table of poems. The front page is everyone's, newest first. Your pen name gets a page with all of your poems, and each poem has its own page. Nothing is counted. Time is the only order, because it cannot be gamed and it does not reward the loudest voice.

Blank lines split stanzas, and single line breaks stay where you typed them. That is the only formatting, since a poem does not need a font picker.

It runs on the web and on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Sign in with Apple, Google, GitHub, X or email. Your avatar is a pixel picture made from your pen name, so there is nothing to upload. Delete your account whenever you like, and every poem goes with it.

The name is Co-Stanza because the plain one was taken on the App Store. Everything is free. Nobody is counting, including me.
