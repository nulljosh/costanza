Every social network wants you to do well

Grow the audience. Hit the numbers. Be somebody. A poem does not care about any of that. It just wants to be left alone on a page. That's the gap.

What Co-Stanza does

You sign in, pick a name, and publish. Each poem gets its own page. Your name gets a page with all of them. The front page is everyone's, newest first. Nobody is counting. That's it. That's the whole product.

Do the opposite

Every instinct says post more, post louder, post for the algorithm. If every instinct you have is wrong, then the opposite would have to be right. So: one poem, one page, no noise.

Everywhere

Web, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Windows and Linux. Same poems, same account.
